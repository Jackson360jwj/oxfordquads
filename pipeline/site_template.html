The forty-three

Every college, and how to get in

Where the University's listing has proved reliable it is the source; where it hasn't, the college's own site is read directly. Each card says which.

Opening times, charges and telephone numbers begin from the University of Oxford's Visiting the colleges page, checked 23 August 2026, then corrected against individual college websites. Colleges are working institutions and close at short notice for exams, services, conferences and building work — the number on each card is the porters' lodge, and a call before you set off is worth it.

How far this has been checked. All 43 entries were reviewed against the University's separate alumni access directory, and 25 colleges checked further against their own websites.

Corrected here: New College (the University understates the concession as £10 — it is £11 — and gives no separate winter Saturday hours), Christ Church (the University publishes neither times nor prices), and Magdalen (a partial closure was missing).

Confirmed accurate at source: Balliol, Brasenose, Magdalen, Trinity and Wadham matched their own pages exactly. Keble, Kellogg, Lady Margaret Hall, St Anne's, St Edmund Hall, Oriel, Worcester and Nuffield agree across both University sources.

Disputed: nine colleges where the University's two published sources contradict each other. They carry a warning on the card; filter for them with "Needs checking".

Contacting the college. Where a college publishes a visitor email or tells visitors to check before arriving, that is shown on its card under "Before you go". A tick beside a phone number means the college's own page publishes that same lodge number — 10 are confirmed that way. Advice is summarised in consistent wording rather than quoted, and the wording of the original is on the college's page.

Still unverified: Jesus, Lincoln, Mansfield, St John's, St Peter's, Somerville and University College. Term dates used to switch between term and vacation hours are approximate. St Stephen's House publishes no visitor hours and is not listed.
