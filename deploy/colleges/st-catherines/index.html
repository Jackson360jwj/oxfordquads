Quick answers

Can you visit St Catherine's College?
No. No tours during the final phases of restoration work. Check the college website in case this changes. The porters' lodge is staffed 24 hours a day for general enquiries.
How much does it cost to visit St Catherine's College?
Free.
Where is St Catherine's College and how do I get there?
St Catherine's College is at Manor Road, Oxford OX1 3UJ, in central Oxford, a short walk from Carfax. Oxford's centre is compact and walkable; use the map link on this page for turn-by-turn directions.
